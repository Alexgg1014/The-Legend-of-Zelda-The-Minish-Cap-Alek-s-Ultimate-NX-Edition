# Alek's Ultimate NX Edition v2.0.2

Bug fixes from the 2.0.1 reports (issues #23 and #24). Thanks @Piznti and
@AngelostKai for the detailed reports, screenshots and save.

Saves and settings carry over; the in-app updater installs it as usual (copy
`tmc_aleks_ultimate_nx.nro` to `/switch/tmc/` as is if installing by hand).

## Fixed

- **Scissors Beetle stood still and never threw its pincers.** The beetle and
  its pincers wrote into each other's data. The same mistake was fixed in four
  other enemy projectiles (Pesto's dirt ball, the soldiers' ball and chain,
  Gyorg's tail, Big Octorok's projectile).
- **Moldorm's body stretched across rooms** and could hit you from another
  room: its segments now follow the head.
- **Daze stars stayed over enemies** after they recovered.
- **People, hanging clothes and doors vanished in Hyrule Town**
  after leaving a house, until you walked away and back.
- **You couldn't enter the waterfalls opened by Kinstone fusions** (Hyrule
  Town and Veil Falls).
- **Widescreen: lighting effects cut off on the right** — the Minish Woods
  light shafts and the darkness/lantern light in dark rooms now reach the
  right edge of the screen.

## Still being looked at

Reports not fixed in this update: Biggoron on Veil Falls, the Wind Tribe
